ℹ️ How to Use TC Config

  1. Check TC Price: Go to your Tibia world and check the current TC market price
  2. Enter Price: Type the TC price in the field above (e.g., 41000)
  3. Optional: Add your character name for reference
  4. Save: Click the "Save TC Settings" button
  5. View Results: Your profit will automatically convert to TC!

💰 Example: If you made 2050kk profit and TC is 41k, that's 50 TC!
